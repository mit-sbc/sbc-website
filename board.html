The SBC Board

The SBC Board is structured to provide industry-specific initiatives working with a club-wide operations group.

Executive Board

Leadership

The executive board consists of presidents and the managing directors of each initiative as well as operations. Operations handles critical administrative tasks, such as marketing for events, organizing large events that cross initiatives, and maintaining the club’s finances.
