Join us · Fall 2026 recruitment

You’re invited to SBC Week.

Sloan Business Club recruits freshmen and sophomores interested in finance, consulting, or entrepreneurship each fall. SBC Week 2026 begins Monday, September 14. Our board is looking forward to meeting you.

How to applyJoin the mailing list
Interested?

View our recruitment schedule below.

You don’t need any business background. If you’re a freshman or sophomore curious about finance, consulting, or entrepreneurship, you’re who SBC Week is for.

Every new member starts in the semester-long Board Education Program, then joins one of the three initiatives.

SBC Week schedule

September 14–18

  1. MonSept 147:30 pm

    SBC President’s Intro & Panel

    Learn all about our organization and hear about how SBC has impacted the business interests and career aspirations of a panel of our members.

    Room 2-190
  2. Mon–FriSept 14–18

    Coffee Chats

    Get to know our board members through informal 1-on-1 coffee chats. Sign-ups go out through the mailing list and are first come, first served.

    Sign up via mailing list
  3. TueSept 157:30 pm

    Business Banter

    Listen in as a group of SBC members speak about their internships and how SBC has helped them navigate their careers.

    Room 2-190
  4. WedSept 167:30 pm

    DEI Social

    Join SBC members for Cookies & Connections and navigate diversity and inclusion at SBC, MIT, and in the workplace. All are welcome.

    Room 10-250
  5. ThuSept 177:30 pm

    Resume Review

    Drop in for 1-on-1 resume feedback from SBC board members. Bring a paper or online copy of your resume.

    Room 2-190
  6. ThuSept 1711:59 pm

    Application due

    Submit your resume and application by 11:59 pm to apply to be a member. The application opens Monday, September 14.

    Online
How to apply

Four steps

Join the mailing list

Get event reminders, coffee chat sign-ups, and the application link the moment it opens. Sign up here.

Come to SBC Week

Meet the board at the Intro & Panel, Business Banter, the DEI Social, and Resume Review. Stop by our booth at Midway too.

Grab a coffee chat

Optional 1-on-1s with board members run Monday through Friday. They are first come, first served.

Submit by Thursday 11:59 pm

Send in your resume and application by Thursday, September 17.

Application

Opens Monday, September 14 and closes Thursday, September 17 at 11:59 pm. The link will appear here when it goes live.
