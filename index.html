About SBC

MIT’s premier undergraduate business organization.

Sloan Business Club is organized across three core initiatives: Finance, Consulting, and Entrepreneurship. New members begin with our intensive, semester-long Board Education Program, designed to build the analytical, strategic, and professional skills needed to succeed in highly competitive business environments.

Upon completing the program, members join one of SBC’s three initiatives, where they deepen their expertise through rigorous industry analysis, hands-on projects, and real-world problem solving. Beyond technical development, SBC provides access to an exceptional network of peers, alumni, and industry professionals, as well as exclusive recruiting opportunities and long-term mentorship that extends well beyond members’ time at MIT.

Initiatives

Three tracks. One community.

Finance

Members in the Finance Initiative participate in mock trading and an educational program comprising the fundamentals of valuation, global markets, economics, and trading. The Finance Initiative also evaluates public equity opportunities to give members practical investing experience.

Learn more

Consulting

The Consulting Initiative connects our members and the general MIT population to the top consulting firms in the industry. Each year, as part of the Consulting Initiative, we partner with a local start-up or VC firm on a strategy or growth project to develop our members in a hands-on setting.

Learn more

Entrepreneurship

The Entrepreneurship Initiative brings founders and industry leaders to campus through its speaker series, and connects members with the resources they need to pursue ventures of their own.

Board Education Program

Where every member starts.

New club members learn fundamental business concepts in finance, consulting, and entrepreneurship during the Board Education Program (BEP). Near the end of the semester, BEP members conduct deep dives and present their projects, which range from stock pitches to start-up business models to strategy consulting.
