Initiative

Consulting

Strategic guidance for real partner companies, and a first-hand look at life as a consultant.

Overview

What we do

The SBC Consulting Initiative provides strategic guidance to partner companies, allowing members to gain a real-world perspective of life as a consultant.

In addition to deliverables, the initiative’s weekly meetings consist of case studies and guest speakers, allowing members to stay informed of current business events. The initiative also connects our members and the general MIT population to the top consulting firms in the industry.

Goals

What members get out of it

  • Ensure members gain valuable experience working on meaningful projects for partner companies.
  • Provide valuable insight into how C-Suite executives address firm-wide challenges.
  • Build a community of leaders prepared to make an impact as they enter the workforce.
Client work

Selected projects

The Consulting Initiative has partnered with venture capital firms, startups, and non-profits on a variety of strategic, technical, and innovative projects. Here are only a few of the companies we have worked with.

Venture capital

SquarePeg Capital

SquarePeg Capital, founded in 2012, is an Australian venture capital firm that focuses on Series A and B investments in Australia, Israel, and Southeast Asia. For SquarePeg Capital, our SBC team conducted due diligence on identified potential investment opportunities in the chatbot industry.

Visit SquarePeg Capital
Venture capital

Bessemer Venture Partners

Bessemer Venture Partners, founded in 1911, is the oldest venture capital firm in the United States, with $4.5 billion under management. For Bessemer Venture, our SBC team identified industries and startups for further investments within full stack robotic integration.

Visit Bessemer Venture Partners
Startup

Posh Development

Posh Development is a Boston-based startup. For Posh Development, our SBC team is identifying new opportunities for market penetration and product integration.

Visit Posh Development
Nonprofit

WaterWells for Africa

WaterWells for Africa is a nonprofit that has been installing water wells in Africa since 1996, providing water for over 26,000 people. For WaterWells for Africa, our SBC team is developing strategic guidelines for crowdfunding online to help WaterWells for Africa continue their work and build their online presence.

Visit WaterWells for Africa
